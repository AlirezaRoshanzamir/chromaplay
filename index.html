Harmonica annotations

Inside a note's <notations><technical>, use:

<other-technical>chromaplay:hole=4;breath=draw;slide=in</other-technical>

Use holes 1-12, blow or draw, and in or out for the slider. The annotation must match the note's pitch. Without one, ChromaPlay chooses a playable fingering. This uses MusicXML's standard extension for custom technique instructions.

      A LITTLE LANGUAGE FOR MUSIC

      From tab to melody.

      Each note is a hole, a breath, and a length. That's it.

      -3#q
      drawholesliderlength
      Tempo
      Write TEMPO 90 before or between notes. It sets the tempo for what follows. Use 30-300 quarter notes per minute. TEMP also works. Default: 60.
      Time signature
      Write TSIG 6/8 wherever the meter changes. It starts a new bar from that point. Default: 4/4. The controls above the score change only the initial settings.
      Breath
      No sign or + to blow. - to draw.
      Hole
      1 through 12. Use 0 for a rest.
      Slider
      Add # after the hole to push the slider in.
      Length
      w whole, h half, q quarter, e eighth, s sixteenth.
      Dotted notes
      A final . adds half the length: 1q. is 1.5 beats.
      Line breaks
      Use uppercase L between notes to start a new row. Rows stay together; scroll sideways for long lines. It adds no time. Example: 1q -1q L 2h 0h.
      Spacing
      Spaces and new lines between notes are optional. Timing commands can share a line with notes. Put a space after TEMPO or TSIG and separate the value from the next note. Use | to make bars easier to read; it adds no time.
      TEMPO 60 TSIG 4/4
      1q -1q 2q -2q
      TEMPO 120 TSIG 6/8 3q. -3q.

      Start at 60 BPM in 4/4, then change to 120 BPM in 6/8. Click a timing box to edit it.

      Tempo always counts quarter notes per minute. Time signature changes the bar lines and metronome accents. C solo tuning follows the HOHNER chart, including D7 for slider-in draw on hole 12. Choose Synthesized reed, FluidR3 harmonica, or VCSL chromatic harmonica in the sound menu.
